🔐 Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Last Updated: 2024

What Data We Collect:

  • Employee ID, Name, Phone Number
  • GPS Location (during work hours only, 9 AM - 6 PM)
  • Visit photos (business-related only)
  • Customer information (name, location)
  • Activity logs & timestamps

How We Use Your Data:

  • Track field work activities
  • Verify visit authenticity
  • Generate performance reports
  • Improve service quality
  • Maintain security & compliance

Data Retention:

Active Data: Kept for 90 days
Backup Archive: Kept for 1 year
Deletion: Securely deleted using DoD 5220.22-M standard

Your Rights (DPDP Act 2023):

  • ✓ Right to access your data anytime
  • ✓ Right to correct inaccurate data
  • ✓ Right to request data deletion
  • ✓ Right to withdraw consent anytime
  • ✓ Right to data portability
  • ✓ Right to lodge complaints

Security Measures:

  • 🔐 AES-256 encryption in transit & at rest
  • 🔐 HTTPS/TLS for all communications
  • 🔐 Role-based access control
  • 🔐 Regular security audits
  • 🔐 Incident response procedures
